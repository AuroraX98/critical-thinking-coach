## Design judgments, not research findings

The approximate 5-8 minute round, one scenario at a time, short feedback, adaptive difficulty, and 0/1/2 observation anchors are practical design choices. The anchors are informal coaching aids, not a validated test. No precise confidence percentage is the official answer to an open-ended case.

A small model simulation can expose answer leakage, arithmetic errors, unfair grading, or excessive length. It cannot establish usefulness for this particular person, lasting learning, or improved judgment outside the session. A stronger follow-up would compare this user's unhinted reasoning on matched new problems before practice, immediately afterward, and at a later session, while recording assistance separately. Even that small personal sample warrants cautious conclusions.
